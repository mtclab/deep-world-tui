# Lore Anchors

What the game asserts about the Deep World, reconciled against the canon
(deep-world-history `src/docs/`) and the published fiction (deep-world-novel).
Where the two disagreed, the published novels win — they are sale-ready and
expensive to change. Reconciliation rulings live in deep-world-history
(`canon_reconciliation` doc; issues #363–#367 there).

## The present

The game opens in **183 AF** — one year after *The Quiet Chronicles* close
(the trilogy runs to 182 AF; the Archive's ethnographic surveys are dated
155 AF, its index compilation 175 AF). AF = After the Fall of the Velkaran
Empire. The status bar and playtest show the year.

**The game year is a compressed abstraction**: 90 days, three 30-day seasons
(Thaw / Green / Frost). Canon's year is 365 days with regional 6- and 8-season
folk calendars; the novels date by month ("the Cold Month") and condition
(melt, flood, trade season). Thaw≈melt, Green≈the growing/trade season,
Frost≈the Cold Month. One game day stands for roughly four canon days; the
lifecycle clock (1 life-year per 3 game days) keeps ages human.

## The Five

Keuru (forests, hospitality — **she**, per the novels), Masa (trade,
perseverance — **she** in Väylä voice), Sampsa (memory, archives — he/neutral),
Oltzed (labor, engineering — he), Kukri (solitude, stillness, winter — he).
The Five withdrew from human company ~11,000 years ago but did not leave;
what magic remains runs on residue, and every craft obeys the Conservation
Principle (the game's craft affinities — word, root, forge, current, still —
are exactly the novels' five crafts).

## Peoples

25 playable/encounterable peoples per the canon naming reconciliation
(Layer-3 endonyms; the charts cite their source docs):
- **6 god-peoples** (Metsik, Arkit, Väylä, Sepät, Ahjo, Laakso) — diaspora
  networks, not states; minorities inside the ~37 territorial polities.
- **14 peoples who stayed** (Varhaiset … Pohjaväki).
- **5 non-human peoples**, with first-encounter patron gods per
  `races/00_sapient_races_overview.md`: Tzäkhar→Kukri (the Deep),
  Mëräk→Keuru+Masa (the tide-line; the game uses Masa), She'ar→Kukri (the
  desert's silence), Häl→Keuru (the canopy), **Khör→Kukri**
  (*Talven-Hiljaisuus*, "Winter's-Silence" — their primary divine
  relationship; they are endothermic steppe herd-followers, at home in
  winter, **not** frost-fleers).
- 'Sharra' is a wiki-layer error for **She'ar** and never appears in the game.

## Festivals

Each people keeps its patron's feast: Harvest Feast & Forge-Day (Oltzed),
Forest Rite & Midsummer Bonfire (Keuru — the Bonfire is the Varhaiset's, the
first people who honor all Five), River Gathering (Masa), **Star-Reckoning**
(Sampsa — star-charts read aloud, the rolls of names recited; the festival of
the Arkit and the memory-peoples), Ancestor Vigil (Kukri — candles and old
names; the festival of the Laakso valleys, the deep folk, the She'ar and the
Khör).

## Game-invented texture (Deep-World-compatible, not canon-sourced)

Companion beasts beyond the canon stock (canon: horse, reindeer, goat, ox,
dog, falcon; game adds biome animals like the cave lurker and tundra fox),
the discovery landmarks, the encounter kinds, and two diseases
(forge-blindness, named for a canon occupation hazard; marsh fever). These
extend the world in its own idiom and may be canonized upstream later.
